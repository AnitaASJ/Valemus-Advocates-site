Sakira Moses is the managing partner of Valemus Advocates. He leads Valemus Advocates with extensive legal experience in commercial, corporate and civil litigation, providing strategic legal advice to businesses and individuals.

PROFESSIONAL PROFILE

About Sakira Moses

Sakira Moses is an advocate of the High Court of Uganda with a background spanning legal practice, public administration, policing and public service.

He holds a Bachelor of Laws degree from Makerere University, obtained in 2016, and a Diploma in Legal Practice from the Law Development Centre, Kampala, obtained in 2017. He was enrolled on the Roll of Advocates of Uganda in 2019.

His legal qualifications were preceded by other academic and professional qualifications, including a Master's degree in Public Administration and Management from Makerere University, obtained in 2004, and a Bachelor of Arts degree in Social Sciences, majoring in Political Science and Sociology, obtained from Makerere University in 1987.

Before transitioning into legal practice, Moses served the Government of Uganda. His experience in public service, policing and administration has contributed to his broad understanding of legal, institutional and public-sector matters.
